The source code of this project is open source under [Apache-2.0](LICENSE). Its names and logos are not: section 6 of the Apache License grants no trademark rights.

## The marks
Vishal Sharma (NewAi.in) uses the following as trademarks: **AI Cinematic Studio**, the planned product name **Spanda**, **Spanda Studio**, **Spanda Pro**, and the associated logos. Trademark registration is being prepared; until then they are unregistered marks.

## What you may do without asking
- Use the names to refer to the project truthfully, for example "built with Spanda Studio" or "a fork of Spanda Studio".
- Use them in articles, tutorials, talks and reviews about the project.
- Keep the names and notices in unmodified copies you redistribute.

## What needs a different name
Forks are welcome, but a **modified** version you distribute must use its own name and logo, and must not suggest that it is the official project or endorsed by it. This includes app names, installer and package names (for example npm scopes or app IDs), domains and social accounts. "Based on Spanda Studio" in your description is fine.

## What is not allowed
- Using the names or logos for a different product or service, or in a way that implies endorsement or partnership.
- Selling "Pro" or add-ons under the Spanda name that are not made by the project.
- Altering the logos.

Questions about a specific use: open a GitHub discussion or issue.
