Spanda · color exploration

Four ways to color the mark

All four use the Kolam S. Each row shows the mark on dark, on light, as an app icon, and how the color behaves inside the studio (primary button, focus ring, selected item). Every pairing shown passes WCAG contrast for its use.

Option 1

Neel (indigo) + Diya

Recommended

The line is indigo, and the two dots are lamps.

"Indigo" comes from the Greek indikón, "from India": the dye was India's gift to the world's color palette. The dots glow like diyas placed on a kolam. Western users see a confident blue-violet with a warm spark; the story is there for anyone who asks.

Strengths

  • Hidden Indian meaning in the color itself, not only the name
  • Stands apart from the violet used by most AI tools
  • Two-color mark is memorable; Diya stays in the logo, not the UI

Trade-offs

  • Needs the studio's accent tokens changed from violet to Neel (small, contained change)
  • On light backgrounds the dots switch to Neel Deep, since Diya is too faint there

Option 3

Haldi (turmeric)

A single warm turmeric gold on near-black, like light from a projector lamp.

The boldest option. Very cinematic and very different from other AI tools, with a quiet Indian root.

Recommendation

Go with Option 1: Neel + Diya

It's the only option where the color carries the same hidden-India idea as the name and the kolam, and it moves Spanda away from the generic AI violet without fighting the studio's status colors. If chosen, the studio's two accent tokens change (violet #7C3AED becomes Neel #4B57E0; lilac #A78BFA becomes Neel Light #8E9AF7). Both keep or improve their contrast.
