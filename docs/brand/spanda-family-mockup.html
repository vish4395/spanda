Small sizes

32px and 16px

At 32px the extra lamps are still clear. At 16px (favicons, menus) the final artwork should drop the extra lamps and rely on the Pro ring or label, since tiny dots turn to noise.

System rules

How a new add-on gets its icon

Same tile, same S

Night rounded square, faint Neel glow, Neel Light line. Never change the S itself.

Extra lamps, one corner

Each add-on gets two to four extra lamps in one free corner of the grid, arranged to hint at its job. Same Diya color as the main dots.

Pro ring

Add-ons carry a thin Neel Light inner ring on the tile. The free studio never has it.

Name in plain English

The icon hints; the name explains. "Scene Library", never a codename.

Considered and rejected

One color per add-on (the Adobe approach)

It's easy to tell apart, but it breaks the lamp story (lamps aren't pink or green), runs out of good colors quickly, and green and amber clash with the studio's "Passed" and "Review required" status colors. It also drifts closer to Adobe's look.
