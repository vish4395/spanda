Logo · colors for growth

Which dot color brings growth?

The line stays Neel. Only the two dots change. Each option is shown with what the color traditionally means in Indian practice, so you can choose with the story in mind.

spanda

Diya amber

#E8A25A · current

The flame of a diya: Lakshmi's lamp, lit at Diwali to invite prosperity and new beginnings. Warm and welcoming.

spanda

Diya gold Recommended

#E9B04C · on Night 9.9:1

Still a lamp flame, nudged toward gold, the color of Guru (Jupiter), the planet of growth, expansion and wisdom in Vedic tradition. It keeps the Lakshmi story and adds the growth one.

spanda

Emerald

#34C38F · on Night 8.6:1

Green is Budh (Mercury), linked to business, trade and intellect, and it means growth almost everywhere. But it loses the lamp story, and in the app it looks like the "Passed" status.

The honest expert view

No color has a measurable effect on luck. What reliably grows a brand is being distinctive (people can tell it apart instantly) and consistent (the same colors, used the same way, for years). Choosing a color with a meaningful story helps you stay consistent, because you'll believe in it. If the traditional view matters to you, the usual route is a reading for the founder or a launch muhurat; that's personal and outside what I can judge.
